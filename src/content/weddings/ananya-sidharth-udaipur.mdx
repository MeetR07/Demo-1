Set against the mirror-like reflections of Lake Pichola and the majestic dome architecture of Udaivilas, Ananya and Sidharth’s wedding was a celebration of timeless heritage and quiet elegance.

From an intimate sun-drenched Mehendi in the palace gardens to an unforgettable Pheras ceremony during golden hour, every moment was shot with cinematic depth and genuine emotion. As dusk fell, hundreds of glowing lanterns lit up the night sky accompanied by Rajasthan’s finest folk musicians.
